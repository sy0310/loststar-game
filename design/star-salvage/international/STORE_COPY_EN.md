# Lostship Star Surge

## Store name

Lostship: Star Surge

## Tagline

Pilot. Evolve. Survive.

## Description

Pilot the Lostship through a 15-minute galactic onslaught. Drag to dodge while your weapons fire automatically. Collect experience, choose upgrades, and build your own combination of weapons and support modules.

Equip up to six active weapons and six support modules. Choose from nine weapons and eight supports. Max out a weapon and its matching support for a chance to evolve it through elite loot. Combine compatible evolved weapons into powerful fusions.

Fight through enemy swarms, distinct elites, and bosses amid pixel-art nebulae. Deploy orbiting crystals, lightning chains, drones, missiles, and more.

Bring back salvage parts to improve your ship and prepare for the next run. Progress and suspended runs are saved locally.

This is an early test version. Feedback on controls, difficulty, weapon combinations, and device performance is welcome.
